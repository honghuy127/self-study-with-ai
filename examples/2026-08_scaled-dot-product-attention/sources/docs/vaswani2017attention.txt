This example study ships a provenance stub instead of a pdftotext extraction
of the paper. The repository does not redistribute paper text, and inventing
an extraction would be exactly the fabrication the workflow exists to prevent.

To make this a real snapshot, fetch the PDF into sources/pdfs/ and run:

    pdftotext -layout sources/pdfs/vaswani2017attention.pdf \
        sources/docs/vaswani2017attention.txt

The note at notes/vaswani2017attention.md anchors every claim to a locator in
the published paper (Section 3.2.1, Equation 1, and footnote 4 on page 4), so
each one can be checked against your own copy without this file. In a real
study the summarizer has no web access and reads this snapshot instead, which
is why the registry requires one.
